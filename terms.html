Reporting and blocking

Every review, comment and profile in altr can be reported: open it and choose Report. Reports go to a moderator, who reviews them and removes content or suspends accounts as needed — the aim is within 24 hours, and always as soon as we can.

You can also block anyone, from their profile. Blocking is mutual and immediate: you stop seeing them and they stop seeing you, in the feed, on the map, in comments and in messages. Blocks can be undone from You → menu → Blocked.

Separately, altr refuses to publish text containing slurs and abuse at the moment you post it. That filter is a first line, not the whole of moderation, and reporting is how anything it misses gets dealt with.
